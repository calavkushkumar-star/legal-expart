Privacy Policy – Legal Expart

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Legal Expart (“we”, “our” or “the app”) provides legal consultancy information and a way for users to request assistance with legal matters and related work. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected and how it may be used.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, we may collect information you provide, such as your name, mobile number, email address, postal address, account details, the details of your legal-service request, and communications you send to us. Please do not submit information that is not necessary for your request.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to create and manage accounts, receive and respond to service requests, communicate with users, provide requested consultancy or related services, track request status, maintain security, and meet applicable legal obligations.

3. Storage and Service Providers

Information may be processed and stored using service providers that support the app, including Firebase services for authentication or data storage where enabled. Such providers may process information on our behalf under their applicable terms and privacy practices.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information. We may share relevant information with authorized personnel who handle your request, service providers necessary to operate the app, or authorities where required by law. We aim to limit sharing to what is reasonably necessary.

5. Data Security and Retention

We take reasonable measures to protect information, but no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, maintain records, resolve disputes, and comply with legal requirements.

6. Your Choices and Requests

You may contact us to ask about your personal information, request correction, or request deletion where applicable. We may need to verify your identity and may retain certain records where the law requires or permits it.

7. Children’s Privacy

The app is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate authorization. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact us.

8. Legal Information

Submitting a request through the app does not by itself create an advocate-client relationship, guarantee that a service will be accepted, or guarantee any legal outcome. Any legal advice or representation is subject to the specific engagement and applicable law.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be published on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.
